EVIDENCE-GROUNDED EMR2A Retrieve · Audit · Defer
Journal article2026

EMR²A

Evidence-Grounded Multimodal Retrieval with Reasoning Audit for Interpretable Pneumonia Subtyping

Xiyao Wang, Liang Liu, Lu Gan, Yang Dai, Miao Zhu, Xihe Qiu, and Leilei Xu

Biomedical Signal Processing and Control, vol. 127, 111011, 2026.

The paper presents an interpretable patient-level framework that jointly encodes chest CT images and structured clinical attributes. It retrieves similar cases for transparent prediction and adds a reasoning-based audit that checks whether the evidence supports the result.

STRUCTURED PRETRAINING VIVID-Med Teach · Transfer · Deploy
arXiv preprint2026

VIVID-Med

LLM-Supervised Structured Pretraining for Deployable Medical ViTs

Xiyao Wang, Xiaoyu Tan, Yang Dai, Yuxuan Fu, Shuo Li, and Xihe Qiu

arXiv:2603.09109, 2026.

The paper uses a frozen language model as a structured semantic teacher for medical vision transformers. Clinical findings become verifiable field-state targets during pretraining; the language model is then removed, leaving a lightweight ViT backbone for deployment.
