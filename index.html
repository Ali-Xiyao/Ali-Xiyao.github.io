Publications

Two publications listed on my Google Scholar profile.
EMR2A: Evidence-Grounded Multimodal Retrieval with Reasoning Audit for Interpretable Pneumonia Subtyping
Xiyao Wang, Liang Liu, Lu Gan, Yang Dai, Miao Zhu, Xihe Qiu, Leilei Xu
Paper  /  Scholar
Biomedical Signal Processing and Control, 127:111011, 2026

A patient-level framework that combines chest CT images with structured clinical attributes, retrieves comparable cases for transparent prediction, and audits whether the retrieved evidence supports the final subtype.

VIVID-Med: LLM-Supervised Structured Pretraining for Deployable Medical ViTs
Xiyao Wang, Xiaoyu Tan, Yang Dai, Yuxuan Fu, Shuo Li, Xihe Qiu
Paper  /  Scholar
arXiv:2603.09109, 2026

A structured pretraining strategy in which a frozen language model teaches clinically meaningful field states to a vision transformer. The language model is removed after pretraining, leaving a lightweight ViT for deployment.
