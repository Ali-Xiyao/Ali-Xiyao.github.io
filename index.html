VIVID-Med: LLM-Supervised Structured Pretraining for Deployable Medical ViTs
Xiyao Wang, Xiaoyu Tan, Yang Dai, Yuxuan Fu, Shuo Li, Xihe Qiu*
Paper  /  Scholar
arXiv:2603.09109, 2026

A structured pretraining strategy in which a frozen language model teaches clinically meaningful field states to a vision transformer. The language model is removed after pretraining, leaving a lightweight ViT for deployment.
